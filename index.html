Privacy Policy

InPlayer

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

Welcome to InPlayer. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how InPlayer handles information when you use our application.

1. About InPlayer

InPlayer is an offline music player application that allows users to play audio files stored locally on their Android devices.

The application does not require users to create an account or register to use its basic music playback features.

2. Information We Access

To provide music playback functionality, InPlayer may access audio files stored on your device, subject to the Android permissions you grant.

Audio files are accessed locally for playback. InPlayer does not upload your personal music files to our servers.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

InPlayer uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share certain information, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Device and advertising identifiers.
  • Information about app and advertisement interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policy and applicable terms.

For more information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy.

You can manage or reset your advertising ID through your Android device settings, where supported.

4. Internet Connection

Music playback is designed to work offline using audio files stored on your device.

An internet connection may be required to load and display advertisements through Google AdMob. Advertising availability does not affect the local music files stored on your device.

5. Data Storage and Security

InPlayer does not operate its own server to store users' personal music files or require personal accounts.

Audio files remain on your device. Information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's data handling and security practices.

6. Data Sharing

InPlayer does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed or shared by Google AdMob as necessary to provide advertising services, analytics, and fraud prevention.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

InPlayer does not maintain a personal user account or a separate server-based music library.

You can remove the application's locally stored data through Android settings or uninstall the application. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's retention and deletion policies.

8. Children's Privacy

InPlayer does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising services may apply their own age-related privacy and consent requirements.

Parents or guardians who have privacy concerns may contact the developer using the contact information below.

9. Third-Party Services

InPlayer uses third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, advertising services, or applicable requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.
